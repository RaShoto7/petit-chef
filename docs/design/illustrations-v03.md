# Illustrations 0.3

Créées avec le tool intégré `image_gen`, sans CLI ni API externe configurée. Les trois PNG sont copiés dans le catalogue d’assets de l’app et fonctionnent hors ligne.

- `Petit Chef/Assets.xcassets/BurgerSketch.imageset/illustration.png`
- `Petit Chef/Assets.xcassets/PastaSketch.imageset/illustration.png`
- `Petit Chef/Assets.xcassets/ToastSketch.imageset/illustration.png`

## Prompt commun

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create a premium French cooking app recipe illustration, a refined loose ink-and-gouache food sketch, expressive thin graphite strokes and colorful watercolor, not a cartoon icon, not photography, no typography, no logos, no characters. Composition as a single cohesive food scene revealed through 4 broad diagonal dry brush swashes running lower left to upper right, with narrow white gaps and irregular bristly edges. The scene remains very readable, not random cut pieces. Lots of clean pure white negative space around the artwork. Crisp warm appetizing color, delicate pencil construction lines escaping paint at edges, sophisticated modern editorial cookbook concept art. Square canvas, main food centered occupies 85% width, no colored rectangular background.

## Sujets ajoutés

**BurgerSketch**

> A beautiful stacked cheeseburger with sesame brioche bun, lettuce tomato cheddar and beef, with a small pile of neatly cut golden French fries to its right. Low three quarter angle, ochre bun, vivid tomato red and fresh lettuce green, warm pencil drawing.

**PastaSketch**

> An elegant shallow white ceramic bowl of spaghetti al limone, tangled yellow pasta with parmesan curls and basil, a half lemon beside it. Three quarter overhead view, sunny yellow and fresh botanical green, loose but accurate fine ink strokes.

**ToastSketch**

> Two slices of rustic toasted sourdough topped with melted mozzarella, diced red ripe tomatoes, basil leaves, a very thin pale ceramic plate. Three quarter overhead view, vibrant red tomato, forest green basil, warm golden bread crust. Beautiful appetizing food drawing.

Les gestes de cuisson sont dessinés et animés séparément dans `FoodIllustration.swift`. Ils ne sont pas des vidéos issues de ces images.
